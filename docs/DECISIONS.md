# Decisions and launch gates

- Product price configured: ₹1,000 (GST-inclusive status not yet confirmed).
- PINs: created only by admin; one-time; free registration authorisation.
- Network: one direct child per member, unlimited depth.
- Commission direction assumption: seller who generated the sale plus eligible upstream members. Confirm this before implementation.
- ₹200 admin allocation is not hard-coded. Calculate contribution margin after GST, discounts, product cost, packaging, shipping, payment fees, returns and fulfilment.
- Live payouts remain disabled pending legal/accounting approval of the direct-selling model.
- Product label, EPA/DHA quantity, serving, allergens, warnings, FSSAI licence and permitted claims must come from approved product documentation.
